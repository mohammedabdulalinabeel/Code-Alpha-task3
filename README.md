## 📖 Overview

This case study explores the integration of **Internet of Things (IoT)** and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** to develop a smart traffic management system.

Rapid urbanization has resulted in increasing traffic congestion, longer travel times, higher fuel consumption, and increased air pollution. Traditional traffic signals generally operate using fixed or semi-fixed timers and do not continuously adapt to real-time traffic conditions.

The proposed **AI-Integrated Smart Traffic Management System** combines IoT-based sensing with AI-based decision-making to monitor traffic conditions and dynamically optimize traffic signal timing.

IoT devices continuously collect traffic information, while AI processes the collected data to identify vehicle counts, estimate congestion, detect incidents, and determine appropriate signal timing.

## 🤖 What is AIoT?

**AIoT (Artificial Intelligence of Things)** refers to the integration of Artificial Intelligence with Internet of Things systems.
    
IoT provides the ability to sense and collect information from the physical environment, while AI analyzes that information and supports intelligent decision-making.

A simple way to understand the relationship is:

IoT → Senses and Collects Data
AI  → Understands and Analyzes Data
